Key Features & Role Access
*The system enforces role-based permissions across four users:*

**♡ Cashier (POS Counter Operations):** Processes customer orders, verifies stock levels in real time, looks up loyalty milestone rewards, calculates totals in Philippine Pesos (₱), and logs transactions.
**♡ Manager (Inventory Control):** Tracks menu items and pricing, monitors automated Low Stock Detector alerts (triggered when stock is <= 5 units), and processes shipment restocks.
**♡ Admin (System Administration):** Manages staff credentials in `users.txt`, broadcasts promotional announcements to customer inboxes, and audits store revenue logs.
**♡ Customer (Loyalty & Messages):** Browses cafe catalog offerings, checks 10-visit loyalty stamp progress, and views inbox announcements.
